Project summary

SpaceGuard AI is an interactive space domain awareness prototype. It combines an orbital visualisation, a collision-risk simulation engine and an event-monitoring dashboard to show how monitoring, risk assessment and decision support can sit in one interface.

What is computed for real

  • Two-body Keplerian propagation for every object, solved with Newton iteration on Kepler's equation.
  • Conjunction screening: separation is sampled every 20 s over the window, then each local minimum is refined to the exact time of closest approach.
  • Collision probability from a simplified isotropic Gaussian model:
    Pc ≈ (R² / 2σ²) · exp(−d² / 2σ²)
    with R the combined object radius, σ the position uncertainty and d the miss distance.
  • Manoeuvres are applied as an impulsive delta-v in the radial, along-track and cross-track frame, converted back to orbital elements and re-screened.

What is simulated

  • The catalogue of about 250 objects is generated from a fixed random seed with realistic orbit families: sun-synchronous, a 550 km shell, navigation orbits, geostationary belt and a debris cloud.
  • Three conjunction scenarios are constructed so the demo has known close approaches. Their secondary objects are marked as scenario objects in the inspector.
  • Anomaly telemetry is scripted. The classifier and the response ranking are rule-based and every rule is shown on screen.

Known limits

  • No J2 oblateness, drag or solar radiation pressure, so positions drift from reality after hours.
  • Uncertainty is a single isotropic number. Real conjunction assessment uses full covariance matrices from tracking data.
  • Altitude uses a spherical Earth. The Earth texture is a graticule only.
  • Nothing here is a trained model, and nothing here is an operational defence system.

Next steps

  • Load real two-line element sets from CelesTrak and propagate with SGP4.
  • Replace the isotropic Pc with a two-dimensional encounter-plane integral using covariance from conjunction data messages.
  • Add J2 and drag, then compare against reference ephemerides.
  • Train an anomaly classifier on labelled telemetry and benchmark it against the rule set shown here.
  • Add multi-satellite constellation planning for avoidance manoeuvres.
